ZS-ERP

Offline accounting, invoicing and inventory software for shops and small businesses on Windows 10/11.

  • Sale and purchase bills with A4 and thermal (80 mm / 58 mm) printing
  • Products and stock, customers and suppliers with ledgers
  • Expenses, profit and 13 stock reports
  • Admin and employee logins with an activity log
  • Daily local backup, and optional backup to your own Google Drive

All business data stays on your own computer. Read the Privacy Policy.

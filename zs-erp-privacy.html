ZS-ERP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

ZS-ERP is an offline accounting, invoicing and inventory software for Windows. This page explains what data the software uses and how.

1. Your business data stays on your computer

All data you enter in ZS-ERP (bills, products, customers, suppliers, expenses, reports) is stored only on your own computer. We do not collect it, and it is not sent to us or to any server owned by us.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, ZS-ERP uploads backup copies of your data to your own Google Drive account, into a folder named “ZS-ERP Backups”. For this the software asks Google for these permissions only:

  • drive.file — to create, read and delete only the backup files that ZS-ERP itself made. ZS-ERP cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.
  • email — to show which Google account is connected.

The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on your computer. Backups are kept in your Google Drive under your control; ZS-ERP keeps the newest 30 and removes older ZS-ERP backups. We (the developer) never receive your Google data, files or password.

ZS-ERP’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Disconnecting

You can click Disconnect in ZS-ERP (Settings & Backup) at any time, or remove access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

4. Licence

To activate the software, you share your computer’s Machine ID with the vendor. This ID is used only to create your licence key.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
